# Act

## Top Recommendations

### 1. Use revenue growth and workforce efficiency together when making hiring decisions

Track revenue QoQ growth, headcount QoQ growth, decoupling gap, and revenue per employee together rather than using headcount growth as the primary indicator of business expansion.

### 2. Shift workforce planning from headcount volume toward skill mix and productivity

When revenue continues to grow without equivalent headcount growth, evaluate whether hiring should focus on critical skills, higher-value capabilities, automation, and productivity rather than simply increasing total employee count.

### 3. Investigate the operational drivers behind the decoupling before making strategic decisions

The analysis identifies a pattern but does not establish its cause. Management should examine utilization, pricing, service mix, subcontracting, acquisitions, employee cost, automation/AI adoption, and deal pipeline to determine what is driving changes in revenue per employee.

## Business Impact of Each Recommendation

| Recommendation | Potential Business Impact |
|---|---|
| **Use revenue growth and workforce efficiency together** | Improves workforce planning by linking hiring decisions to business growth and efficiency rather than headcount alone. |
| **Focus on skill mix and productivity** | Helps organizations allocate hiring budgets toward capabilities that support revenue growth while avoiding unnecessary workforce expansion. |
| **Investigate the drivers of decoupling** | Helps distinguish productivity improvements from other factors such as demand changes, pricing, acquisitions, or changes in workforce composition before taking action. |

## Connection to the Analysis

The selected peer group shows that revenue and headcount growth did not move together in the same way during Period B.

TCS's average decoupling gap increased from **1.21 pp in Period A to 2.01 pp in Period B**, an improvement of **+0.80 pp**. HCLTech and Infosys showed larger improvements, while Wipro moved in the opposite direction.

Therefore, the appropriate business response is not simply to reduce hiring. Instead, organizations should investigate what is driving the changing relationship between revenue and workforce size and align future workforce decisions with the underlying business and productivity drivers.

## Key Action for Future Analysis

To strengthen the analysis, additional operational data could be incorporated, including:

- Employee utilization rates
- Employee cost and cost per employee
- Subcontractor workforce and spending
- Revenue mix and pricing
- Acquisition-related revenue
- Attrition by skill or employee segment
- AI and automation adoption
- Deal pipeline and demand indicators

This would help determine **why** revenue is becoming less dependent on proportional headcount growth rather than only identifying **whether** the relationship is changing.
