# Ask

## Business Task
Analyze how TCS's revenue, headcount, attrition, and profitability trends compare
to its closest industry peers — Infosys, Wipro, and HCLTech — over the past 5 years,
to determine whether TCS is decoupling revenue growth from headcount growth faster
or slower than the rest of the Indian IT-services sector, and what that signals about
the traditional linear "more headcount = more revenue" hiring model.

## Context
Through FY26, TCS reduced its workforce by over 23,000 employees while revenue
continued to grow, prompting public debate about whether AI-driven productivity
gains, a genuine demand slowdown, or cost discipline are reshaping the IT-services
business model. This project treats that debate as a testable question rather than
a headline, using publicly reported financial and workforce data across TCS and its
three closest listed peers.

## Key Stakeholders
- IT-services industry analysts and investors tracking sector-wide hiring trends
- HR and workforce-planning teams at IT-services firms benchmarking against peers
- Job seekers and current employees trying to understand whether the "hiring pyramid"
  model is structurally shrinking

## Guiding Questions
1. How do TCS's revenue, headcount, attrition rate, and EBIT margin trend quarter
   over quarter compared to Infosys, Wipro, and HCLTech over the last 5 years?
2. Which company has decoupled revenue growth from headcount growth fastest,
   measured by revenue-per-employee?
3. Is TCS an outlier in the sector, or is the entire "WITCH" peer set moving the
   same direction at a similar pace?
4. Does margin expansion across these companies correlate more strongly with
   headcount reduction or with revenue growth?

## Scope
- Time period: last 5 fiscal years (adjust once you confirm how far back clean
  quarterly data is available for all four companies)
- Companies: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech
- Out of scope: stock price movement, employee sentiment/reviews, job postings —
  reserved for a possible follow-up analysis

## Deliverables
1. A clear statement of the business task (this document)
2. A description of all data sources used
3. Documentation of data cleaning/transformation steps
4. A summary of the comparative analysis
5. An interactive Tableau dashboard with supporting visualizations
6. Top 3 recommendations/insights based on the analysis
